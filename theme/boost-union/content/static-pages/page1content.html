Welcome to Bosonance (bosonance.com). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of students, parents, and aspirants using our online test series and digital self-study portal.

1. Information We Collect

To provide access to our NEET, JEE Main, JEE Advanced, and WBJEE test series, we collect:

  • Account Data: Name, email address, phone number, state, and target exam tier selected during registration.
  • Performance Data: Quiz responses, test attempt timestamps, score reports, test completion history, and rank standings.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, browser information, and login timestamps to ensure secure account access.
  • Payment Details: Online payments are processed via secure third-party payment gateways. Bosonance does not store credit/debit card details or UPI PINs on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely for educational and platform administration purposes, including:

  • Enabling account login and managing course subscriptions.
  • Generating performance analytics, test progress reports, and solution reviews.
  • Sending critical course updates, schedule announcements, and password reset links.
  • Ensuring platform security and preventing unauthorized account sharing.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

Bosonance maintains a strict privacy pledge:

  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade student personal data to third-party advertisers.
  • Data is shared only with trusted infrastructure providers (e.g., web hosting servers and payment processing gateways) necessary to operate the platform.

4. Cookies

Bosonance uses essential session cookies required by Moodle to maintain user logins during active test sessions and secure navigation across course modules.

5. Student Rights & Data Access

Students can request a copy of their stored account data or request profile deletion by contacting our helpdesk at support@bosonance.com.
